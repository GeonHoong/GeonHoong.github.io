About

I am an undergraduate student in Mechanical & Automotive Engineering at the University of Ulsan and an undergraduate researcher at the Human Robotics Lab (HRlab), advised by Prof. Kyoung-Soub Lee.

My research focuses on wearable and soft robotic systems that reduce physical load in industrial settings such as shipyards — from cable-driven upper-limb assistance to pneumatic gloves for grip assistance and tool-vibration damping. I work across mechanical design, fabrication, and embedded control.

InnoCORE Research Internship · Jun – Sep 2026

Active Vibration Damping via Pneumatic Air-Chamber Optimization

Investigated air-chamber designs to reduce vibration transmitted from handheld tools to the hand. Fabricated Zero-volume Air Chamber (ZAC) and Dielectric Elastomer Actuator (DEA) prototypes as alternative actuation mechanisms.

Role: ZAC/DEA prototyping, air-chamber fabrication, IMU-based vibration measurement, embedded pneumatic actuation.
