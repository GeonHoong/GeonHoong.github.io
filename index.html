About

I am an undergraduate researcher in the Human Robotics Lab at the University of Ulsan, advised by Prof. Kyoung-Soub Lee. My interests lie in wearable robotics, soft robotic mechanisms, human–robot interaction, and embedded control systems.

I design and prototype wearable devices by combining mechanical structures, electronic circuits, and sensor-based control. My current work includes cable-driven assistance for shipboard cable installation and pneumatic systems for reducing hand-transmitted tool vibration.

I plan to pursue graduate studies in wearable and soft robotics, focusing on mechanical design, sensing, and control. I am particularly interested in developing robotic systems that can be evaluated through hands-on prototyping and experimental measurement.

Human Robotics Lab, University of Ulsan · Dec 2025 – Present

Cable-Driven Wearable Robot for Shipboard Cable Installation

This ongoing project investigates a wearable assistive mechanism for upper-limb-intensive cable-installation tasks in shipbuilding. The system uses cable-driven actuation to provide pulling assistance while considering wearability and integration with the worker's movement.

My contributions — wearable mechanism and control-box design, hardware/software integration, and CAN-based motor communication and control.

  • Mechanical: cable routing, pulley-based actuation layout, and wearable assembly design.
  • Embedded: motor-controller integration and control testing via CAN communication.
  • Current focus: system integration, usability-oriented mechanical refinement, and prototype validation.

HRlab · Apr–Jun 2026  |  InnoCORE, UoU–UNIST · Jun–Sep 2026

Active Pneumatic Vibration-Damping Glove

I developed a pneumatic-glove prototype intended to reduce vibration transmitted between handheld tools and the user's hand. The prototype uses grip detection and IMU-based vibration analysis to inform air-chamber actuation.

During the InnoCORE internship, I separately explored pneumatic air-chamber geometry and fabricated Zero-volume Air Chamber (ZAC) and Dielectric Elastomer Actuator (DEA) prototypes to investigate alternative soft-actuation concepts.

My contributions — glove and chamber fabrication, IMU vibration RMS processing, pressure-sensor-based grip detection, and embedded pneumatic control.
