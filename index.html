About

I am a senior undergraduate at the University of Ulsan and a member of the Human Robotics Lab led by Prof. Kyoung-Soub Lee. My work sits at the intersection of mechanical design, soft actuation, and embedded control: I design and fabricate wearable devices, build the electronics and firmware that drive them, and measure how they change the load on the human body.

Much of my research is motivated by shipbuilding — overhead cable installation and long hours with vibrating handheld tools — where a well-designed wearable can make a real difference to workers.

HRlab, University of Ulsan · Dec 2025 – Present

Cable-Driven Wearable Robot for Shipboard Cable Installation

Installing heavy cables overhead on a ship's deck is one of the most physically demanding tasks in shipbuilding. I am developing a cable-driven wearable robot that supports the upper limbs during this work, reducing muscular effort while keeping the device light enough to wear through a shift.

My role — mechanical design, HW/SW integration, CAN-based motor communication and control.

HRlab & InnoCORE Internship (UoU–UNIST) · Apr 2026 – Sep 2026

Active Pneumatic Vibration-Damping Glove

Hand-transmitted vibration from power tools is a long-term health hazard. I designed a pneumatic glove whose air chambers adapt their pressure based on grip and vibration sensing, then extended the work during the InnoCORE internship by studying chamber geometry and fabricating Zero-volume Air Chamber (ZAC) and Dielectric Elastomer Actuator (DEA) prototypes as alternative actuation mechanisms.

My role — glove prototyping, air-chamber fabrication, IMU-based vibration RMS analysis, pressure-sensor grip detection, embedded pneumatic actuation.
